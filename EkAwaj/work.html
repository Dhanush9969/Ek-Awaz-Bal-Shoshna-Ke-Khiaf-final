Our Strategic Pillars

Three interlocking pillars — working in concert to build lasting change.

01
Pillar One

Prevention & Awareness

We run school-based programs teaching children about body autonomy, safe/unsafe touch, and the importance of speaking up. Our "Good Touch Bad Touch" curriculum reaches over 1,000 schools annually across Punjab.

We also run parent sensitisation workshops, community awareness drives, and train local leaders and health workers to identify and report abuse.

School curriculum integration (Grades 3–10)

02
Pillar Two

Protection & Rescue

Our trained social workers work directly with survivors, providing immediate crisis support, legal aid navigation, and coordination with law enforcement under POCSO guidelines.

We partner with Ludhiana Police, Child Welfare Committees, and District Legal Services Authorities to ensure survivors receive justice swiftly and compassionately.

24/7 crisis intervention hotline
Legal aid & POCSO case guidance

03
Pillar Three

Healing & Recovery

Trauma does not define a child's future. Our certified counsellors provide long-term psychosocial support — helping survivors rebuild confidence, trust, and hope.

We believe healing is not a destination but a journey. Every child deserves the time, space, and professional support to walk that path surrounded by care.

Certified child trauma counselling
Family therapy & parent support groups
Art & play-based therapy for young children
School reintegration programs
